# LifeRing, by Yuriy Neyra Alvarado and Taras Vakulenko
A futuristic idea app of what healthcare tech might look like in space through the future. 

Combining aspects of modern minimalism and information presentation, the app is combined with wireless capabilities (simulated through pings to a secondary device through pinging over a network) to provide live "simulated" health data to present to the user.

The app was coded (with AI assistance) at the Liftoff hackathon sponsored by Nokia and Thales, with the track of a futuristic setting where a certain problem with lifestyle must be addressed as people travel through space. The app, titled LifeRing, aims to resolve two angles of a healthcare aspect in space: understanding your own health and risks, and being able to present your data to a professional that can analyze your healthcare data to provide better diagnoses and treatments. The app is able to switch between a simplified user mode and a more quantitative analytical mode for professionals in order to provide full healthcare functionality to anyone that might need it.

The UI/UX was laid out beforehand to demonstrate the futuristic feeling, while the real code was constructed in Kotlin with the assistance of Gemini to make quick modifications and correct imperfections within the time constraints. The product was pitched and received positive reviews from all judges that we presented to, with the only recommendation being that we were to include a feature for user data to be fully exported (through encryption algorithms).
